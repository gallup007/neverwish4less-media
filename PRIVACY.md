# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This policy covers the personal tools used by the owner of the "Never Wish For Less Time" accounts (@neverwish4less) on Pinterest, Threads, Instagram and Facebook, including the apps "NWFLT Cleanup" and "NWFLT Publisher".

## What these tools do

- They publish the owner's own posts to the owner's own accounts.
- They read engagement statistics (such as likes, saves, clicks and comment counts) for the owner's own posts.
- They remove the owner's own posts that received no engagement.

These tools are used only by the account owner. They are not offered to anyone else.

## Data

- No data about any other person is collected, stored, sold or shared.
- The only data handled is the owner's own posts and the statistics the platforms report about them.
- Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are never shared.
- Copies of removed posts may be kept privately by the owner for their own records.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to @neverwish4less on any of the platforms above.
